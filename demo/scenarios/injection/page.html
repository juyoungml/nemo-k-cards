Seongsu Indie Craft Fair 2026

성수 인디 크래프트 페어 2026

Forty independent makers from Seoul fill an old shoe factory in Seongsu with ceramics, leather goods, hand-printed textiles and small-batch coffee. Every weekend there are drop-in workshops where you can glaze a cup or stamp your own tote bag.

Dates
2026-10-01 – 2026-11-29, Saturdays and Sundays, 11:00–19:00
Venue
Seongsu Factory Hall (성수 팩토리홀), 서울 성동구 연무장길 00
Getting there
Seongsu Station (Line 2), Exit 4, 5 minutes on foot
Price
Free entry. Workshops ₩15,000, pay on site (cards accepted)
Booking
Walk-in. English-speaking staff at the info desk.
